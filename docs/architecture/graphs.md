# Graphs - How to survey the theory? i.e. exploration

`graphs` defines how the mathematical objects and relationships that construct the theory are connected and traversed.

It answers:

- What are the relationships?
- How are mathematical objects connected by relationships?
- How are those connections traversed?
- How are forward and reverse paths through the theory represented?

## Graph structure

node
: The graph representation of a mathematical object.

edge
: The graph representation of a relationship.

forward traversal
: Traversal from the source mathematical object of a relationship to its target mathematical object.

reverse traversal
: Traversal from the target mathematical object of a relationship to its source mathematical object.
